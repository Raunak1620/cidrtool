How this speed test works

The test runs in three steps against Cloudflare's public speed test endpoints, which are served from the edge location nearest to you.

  1. Latency and jitter. It sends a series of empty requests and records how long each round trip takes. Latency is the median, and jitter is the average difference between consecutive results. The first request is ignored because it includes the time to open the connection.
  2. Download. It opens four parallel connections, downloads data for eight seconds and ignores the first second while TCP ramps up. Speed is the bytes received divided by the time.
  3. Upload. It sends data over three parallel connections for six seconds and measures how much was accepted.

Reading the results

  • Mbps versus MB/s. Speeds are quoted in megabits per second. A 500 Mbps line downloads at most about 62 megabytes per second.
  • Latency. Under 30 ms is good for most things, and under 20 ms is ideal for gaming. Wi-Fi and distance to the server both add to it.
  • Jitter. Jitter above roughly 30 ms affects voice and video calls even when the average latency is fine.

Why your result may be lower than your plan

  • Wi-Fi. Test on a wired connection to measure the line, not the wireless link.
  • Other traffic. Backups, streaming and other devices share the same line.
  • Distance and routing. The test measures the path to a nearby server. A single download from a far-away site can be slower, as explained in the bandwidth-delay product guide.
  • Browser limits. A browser test is accurate to a few hundred Mbps and can under-report multi-gigabit links, because the browser and CPU become the limit.
  • VPNs and ad blockers. A VPN adds its own route and overhead, and some blockers stop the test from reaching the server.

Frequently asked questions

How much data does the test use?

On a fast connection it can use 100 to 200 MB or more, because it runs for a fixed time and not a fixed size. Slow connections use less. Avoid running it on a metered mobile connection.

Is my data sent to this website?

No. Your browser connects directly to Cloudflare's speed test servers, and the results are calculated on your device.

Why do other speed tests show different numbers?

Different tests use different servers, numbers of connections and durations. Results from the same test on the same network are the best to compare.

What is a good speed?

For one person streaming HD video, about 10 Mbps is enough, and 4K needs around 25 Mbps. A household with several users benefits from 100 Mbps or more, and upload matters for video calls and cloud backups.
