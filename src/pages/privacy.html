Summary

{{SITE}} ({{DOMAIN}}) provides free network tools. We do not require an account and we do not ask for personal information. This page explains what data is processed when you use the site.

Data you enter into tools

The CIDR calculator, subnet chart, IP converter and bandwidth calculator run in your browser; what you type is not sent to us. When you use a lookup tool, your browser sends the query you entered directly to the relevant third-party service, as described below. We do not receive or store those queries on our own servers.

Third-party services used by the tools

  • What is my IP: your browser requests your public IP address from ipify (api.ipify.org and api64.ipify.org) and location and network details from ipwho.is, with ipapi.co as a fallback. These services necessarily receive your IP address.
  • DNS lookup: queries go to Cloudflare (cloudflare-dns.com) or Google Public DNS (dns.google) over HTTPS, according to the resolver you pick. They receive the name you query and your IP address.
  • BGP and ASN lookup: queries go to the RIPEstat data API (stat.ripe.net). It receives the ASN, IP or prefix you query and your IP address.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy that governs how they handle requests.

Cookies, local storage and advertising

We store one item in your browser's local storage (your light or dark theme choice). It stays on your device.

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads are shown in line with applicable consent requirements, and you may be asked for your consent by a consent management platform.

Server logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, requested page, browser type and time) for security and operations.

Children

The site is intended for a general technical audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not collect personal information through the tools, there is generally nothing for us to retrieve, but you can contact us with any request.
